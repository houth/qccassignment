The Northern Light



Iceland is a hiker’s paradise, with a vast network of trails in lowland and highland areas. Whether seeking comfortable nature trails or challenging multi-day adventures in remote, untouched wilderness, you will find various options. You can pick trails that run across mountains, along valleys or rugged coastlines, over moss-grown lava fields, and explore waterfalls, hot springs, volcanoes, and glaciers, to name a few highlights. You will find various hiking routes around Iceland, and each part of the country has its specialty and unique nature gems.
